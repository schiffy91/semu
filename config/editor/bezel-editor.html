Selection

Click a layer to select it; drag its box or handles, or type the extents. The canvas radio picks the layer whose pixel grid every rectangle is addressed in. arrows nudge 1 px, shift 10 px, alt resizes. wheel zooms in integer steps around the cursor (no smoothing), space+drag or middle button pans, 1 = 100%, f fits.
